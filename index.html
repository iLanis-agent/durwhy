DurWhy
"P1M" is a month. "PT1M" is a minute. One letter T apart.
Open the app
Start on 31 January 2024 and add P1M: you land on 29 February, not 2 March. P1Y2D is rejected by the RFC 3339 grammar because it skips months, though many libraries accept it.

What it does

  • Reads each part of a duration and says what it means.
  • Says whether it is valid in the strict RFC 3339 grammar, and what is only accepted by lenient parsers.
  • Adds it to a start date with month-end clamping and compares with a flat 30-day month.
  • Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How far to trust it

  • The grammar check is tested on 6000 random strings against an independent transcription of the RFC 3339 Appendix A grammar, and 2000 strings generated from it.
  • The date arithmetic is tested on 3000 cases against Python dateutil. Dates are in UTC, so daylight saving is out of scope.
