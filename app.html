Type an ISO 8601 duration and a start. See what it means and where it lands.
Dates use UTC, so there are no daylight-saving jumps here. In a real time zone a day can be 23 or 25 hours. Years and months are applied first with the day clamped to the month length, then weeks, days and time as exact elapsed time. Strict means it matches the RFC 3339 Appendix A grammar. Fractions are accepted when they sit on the last component; "." or "," both work.
